# Google Play listing — PauseCart

Package: `com.PauseCartMNG.PauseCart`

## Title
PauseCart

## Short description
Park a want on a shelf and decide only when the wait ends.

## Full description
PauseCart is a shelf for things you really want to buy now, but want time to think. Type the name, the expected price, and an optional link. Choose a wait of one day, three days, one week, or a date you pick. Write why you want it, and whether you already own something similar.

When the clock ends, choose still want, later, or let it go. Later starts the wait again. Items you dropped stay in a list, and their prices are not called money saved. Nothing is ordered.

Share a link into the app, or paste one. PauseCart does not open the store page. Reminders stay on this phone. No account.

PauseCart Pro keeps more than 8 waiting items. Looks and other extras unlock with coins. Remove ads and Pro are one-time unlocks.

Remote config: https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N307.json — if disable=1, Play purchase sheets stay hidden. The shop still spends coins.
