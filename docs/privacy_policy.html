Privacy Policy – PauseCart

Last updated: October 2026

PauseCart is a private, on-device waiting shelf. Item names, prices, links, reasons, decisions, coins, and shop unlocks stay on this phone. We do not create an account, and we do not fetch product pages from stores.

Data stored on device

  • Items you save, why you wanted them, and the decision you made
  • Coins, owned shop items, and the active look
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and PauseCart Pro

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and PauseCart Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N307.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

We do not sell personal data. Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
